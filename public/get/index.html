Installing on Android

  1. Tap a download above on the phone itself.
  2. Android will warn about installing outside the Play Store — choose Settings and allow this browser to install apps.
  3. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.

Every app here is a thin shell around this website, so everything we add reaches your phone without reinstalling. A new download is only needed when the app itself changes — a new name, a new icon, a new address.
